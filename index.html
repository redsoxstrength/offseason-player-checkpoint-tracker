Checkpoint Call Script

Goal: surface real, actionable insight — not a one-word "all good." Use these as prompts, not a rigid script; follow up on anything vague.

1. Training status & adherence

  • Walk me through the last two weeks session by session — what did you actually get in versus what was programmed?
  • Where did you have to modify, shorten, or skip something, and why?
  • How are you rating effort/quality on your key lifts and conditioning pieces (not just "did it happen")?

2. Bodyweight trend

  • What's your weight done since the last check-in, and how does that compare to your target?
  • What do you think is driving that direction — nutrition, appetite, travel, stress, training volume?
  • Are you actually tracking it (scale, frequency), or estimating?

3. What's working / what needs adjusting in the remote program

  • Which parts of the program are clicking, and which feel like they're not doing much for you?
  • Any exercise you're avoiding, modifying heavily, or not confident performing on your own?
  • Is the volume/intensity landing right, or does something need to move up or down?

4. Resource and access gaps

  • What do you not have access to right now (equipment, facility, coaching eyes) that the program assumes you do?
  • Is anything about your current setup forcing a workaround we should know about?
  • Do you need us to adjust programming around what's actually available to you?

5. Goal check

  • Where do you feel you stand against your return-to-camp goals right now?
  • Does anything about those goals need to change based on how the last two weeks went?
  • What's the one thing you want to be true by the next check-in?

Close

  • Confirm any programming adjustments you're making as a result of this call.
  • Tell them when the next check-in is.
  • Post the Slack update immediately after — while it's fresh.

Slack Post Script & Process

One initial post per player, in their channel. Every later update is a threaded reply underneath it — never a new top-level post.

Initial post template (post once, at the start of the off-season)

  • End-of-season status: health status leaving 2026 (full health / managing something / rehab), affiliate, and role context.
  • Off-season focus areas: the 2–3 physical priorities for this player's off-season.
  • Return goals: what "on track for Spring Training" looks like for this player specifically — body comp, output markers, health status.
  • Programming key points: the non-negotiables in the plan — anything a covering coach needs to know at a glance.

Threading convention

  • Every subsequent call's notes go in as a reply on that original post — same thread, never a new message in the channel.
  • Lead each reply with the date and window number (e.g. "Window 4 – Jan 10").
  • Keep it scannable: adherence, weight trend, one line on program fit, one line on goal status, any adjustment made.
  • Detailed call notes live here, not in the tracker — the tracker only records that the call and the post both happened.
